What counts as a leak?

Full methodology →
1

Advance leaks & digital distribution

Master question papers extracted from bank vaults, printing facilities, or courier transit and circulated across WhatsApp or Telegram prior to test commencement. Verified matches are catalogued.

2

Electronic solver rings & cyber compromise

Micro-Bluetooth transmitters, customized earpieces, vanishing-ink pens, and remote desktop hijacking inside private computer labs streaming live answer keys during examinations.

3

Institutional tampering & score fabrication

Post-exam OMR sheet replacement, correction fluid alterations, engineered seating charts for proxy impersonators, and database score manipulation verified by judicial or police inquiries.
